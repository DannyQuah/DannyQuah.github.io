excerpt: |
  This policy paper examines how and why the original five BRICS countries (Brazil, Russia, India, China, and South Africa) have in recent years explored the use of their own currencies or shared currency and payments systems, and how they could realize this. Drawing on the theory of international money, it assesses the feasibility of these initiatives, and their potential consequences, analyzing the proposed institutional designs and piloting for a shared currency and payments system, including the R5, the UNIT, BRICS Pay, BRICS Bridge, and BRICS Clear.
