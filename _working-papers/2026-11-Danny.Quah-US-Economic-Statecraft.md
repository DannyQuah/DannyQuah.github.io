excerpt: |
  When Great Powers exercise economic statecraft, how should other nations respond?  Traditional hypotheses include alignment or generalised hedging or some combination in between.  This paper suggests instead that nations use strategies of acquiescence, adaptation, aggregation, or resistance (3A+ for short). By operationalising these strategies' conceptual underpinnings the paper establishes 3A+ empirical significance in (1)~strategic differentiation; (2)~dynamic complementarity; and (3)~Great Power elasticity. For its empirical analysis the paper develops a new database of geoeconomic response dynamics. Besides formally defining the key concepts of 3A+, the paper's analytical formulation operationalises resilience and economic security, two concepts of independent interest in geoeconomics.
